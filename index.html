01About

From signal to system

My path began in telecommunications, where every system is a matter of signals, feedback and delay: how networks stay stable when information arrives late and countless nodes act at once.

Today I bring that discipline to artificial intelligence. Modern AI agents are feedback systems too: they read shared state, act and write back, with latency at every step. Over 15 years across telecom networks, IT and software, I have architected and built AI platforms end to end, from system architecture and backend to web, mobile and multi-provider LLM integration.

Alongside engineering, I am an independent researcher. I apply control theory to understand when intelligent systems converge, oscillate or fail, publish the results, and release open-source tools that others can verify. My MBA connects this work to strategy, change management and operations, so that AI becomes part of how an organisation works rather than an experiment beside it.
